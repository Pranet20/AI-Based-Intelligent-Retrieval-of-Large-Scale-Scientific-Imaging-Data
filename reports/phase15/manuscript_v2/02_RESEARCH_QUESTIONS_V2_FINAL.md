# 2. Research Questions (Final V2 Manuscript Draft)

This investigation is organized around four central research questions addressing representation learning, multimodal fusion, cross-domain generalization, and human curation workflows:

- **RQ1 (Visual Representation Trade-offs):** How do self-supervised Vision Transformers compare against established supervised convolutional neural network architectures in fine-grained microstructural retrieval across variable microscope acquisitions?
- **RQ2 (Multimodal Conditioning & Confounder Dynamics):** Can incorporating standardized instrument acquisition metadata (voltage, current, magnification, detector mode) into visual representation spaces improve retrieval accuracy, or does metadata act as an empirical confounder?
- **RQ3 (Cross-Domain & Cross-Modality Generalization):** To what extent do self-supervised representations calibrated on specific metallurgical regimes generalize to distinct materials domains (e.g. industrial semiconductor defects) and transmission microscopy (TEM) modalities?
- **RQ4 (Uncertainty & Human Curation Workflow):** Can principled latent density estimation reliably discriminate retrieval correctness, and can composite algorithmic prioritization reduce expert curation workload in repository maintenance?
