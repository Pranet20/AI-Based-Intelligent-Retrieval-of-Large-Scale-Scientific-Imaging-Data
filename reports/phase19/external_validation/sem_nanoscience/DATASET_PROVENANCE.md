# DATASET PROVENANCE: SEM IMAGES FOR NANOSCIENCE

**Dataset Name**: Scanning Electron Microscopy Images for Nanoscience (100% Consensus Subset)  
**Publication Source**: Nature Scientific Data (2018)  
**Publication DOI**: 10.1038/sdata.2018.172 (Author Correction: 10.1038/sdata.2019.18)  
**Repository**: Figshare / Open Science Framework  
**License**: CC BY 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International)  
**Total Images**: 21,169 micrographs  
**Modalities**: Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) with secondary electron (SE) and backscattered electron (BSE) detectors  
**Morphological Categories**: 10 primary classes (biological, fibres, films_coated_surface, mems_devices_and_electrodes, nanowires, particles, patterned_surface, porous_sponge, powder, tips)  
**Independence Status**: `INDEPENDENT_EXTERNAL`  
**Prior Project Usage**: Zero usage in Phases 1 through 17 (neither in training, validation, nor testing).
