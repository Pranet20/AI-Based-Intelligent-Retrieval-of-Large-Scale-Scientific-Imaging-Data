# IEEE ISBI 2027 Venue Compliance Checklist

**Conference:** 2027 IEEE 24th International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging (ISBI 2027)  
**Official Conference URL:** `https://biomedicalimaging.org/2027/`  
**Author Instructions:** `https://biomedicalimaging.org/2027/papers/`  
**Official Paper Deadline:** 26 October 2026  

---

## 1. Page Budget & Formatting Strictness

- **Format:** IEEE 2-column conference format (IEEEtran style).
- **Page Budget:** Exactly **4 pages** for all technical content, including Title, Abstract, Text, Figures, Tables, and In-Text Citations.
- **Fifth Page Policy:**
  - A 5th page is permitted **strictly and exclusively** for non-technical material:
    - Compliance with Ethical Standards
    - Acknowledgments / Conflicts of Interest
    - References
  - Technical overflow (e.g., placing Results, Tables, or Discussion on page 5) will result in immediate desk rejection.
  - The 5th page incurs an additional fee upon acceptance.

---

## 2. Ethical Compliance Standards

Per official IEEE guidelines, authors must state compliance with ethical standards:
- **Verified Study Phrasing:**
  > *"This study utilized publicly available scientific imaging datasets (HCCI bearing steel, Carinthia SEM, and BBBC021 bioimage benchmark) and controlled synthetic augmentations. The research did not involve human subjects, patient data, clinical interventions, or live animal experiments, and therefore did not require institutional review board (IRB) approval."*
- **Avoid Unsupported Wording:** Do NOT state *"No institutional review was required"* without explicitly stating that the data consisted solely of publicly available open datasets and synthetic images.

---

## 3. Generative AI Disclosure Guidelines

Per IEEE Author Guidelines:
- The use of AI-driven tools (e.g., LLMs) for basic grammatical editing and language polishing is permissible without formal authorship status.
- If AI systems contribute substantially to code generation, analysis framing, or literature summarization, an explicit acknowledgment must be included in the Acknowledgments section:
  > *"The authors acknowledge the use of agentic software development assistants for code refactoring, test suite synthesis, and manuscript drafting assistance."*
- Final responsibility for all claims, references, and mathematical correctness remains entirely with the human authors.
